The Lighthouse Keeper's Log

The North Point lighthouse was first lit in 1871 and was automated in 1989. Its lamp is visible for 22 nautical miles on a clear night.

The last resident keeper, Margaret Ellery, kept a handwritten log for nineteen years. Her final entry records a storm with waves that reached the gallery deck, forty metres above the sea.

Today the tower is maintained by the coastal trust, which opens it to visitors on the first Sunday of every month. The climb is 214 steps.
